# Telning Design System
## One look for every book, page and post (working document)

- Version: 2.0, 5 October 2026. Replaces v1.0 (kept as `telning-design-system-v1.md`).
- Goes with: the project brief and `telning-research-guideline.md`. The guideline says why. This file says how.
- Big changes since v1: a human illustrator draws everything; age is 3–7; the book goes worldwide in several language editions; the paper decision is made; an illustrator brief and a rights section are added.

---

## 1. Summary

### Problem
Our books cost more than the €6 books next to them, and Amazon prints all of them on the same paper. The cover must show the value at the size of a stamp on a phone. Now the pages must also work for a 3-year-old who scribbles, and the same pictures must sell in every language.

### Solution
A calm brand base (cream, charcoal, sage) plus one strong colour per book. Pim is large on every cover and half coloured in. One bold black line everywhere, drawn by hand. All text sits on cream or white, and all text lives on its own layer so a language edition changes only words, never pictures.

### Outcome
A parent sees in one second: for small children, about a feeling, a colouring book, hand-drawn, and which book it is. Five books look like one family. A new language edition takes days, not weeks.

---

## 2. Design rules (the short list)

1. **One second.** Every cover works at 160 px wide on a white page.
2. **One colour per book.** The parent finds the book by colour first, title second.
3. **The black line is the brand.** The same bold hand-drawn line is on the cover, inside, on the story card and on every post.
4. **Pim is white.** The child gives Pim a colour. On covers, Pim is half coloured to show this.
5. **Text only on cream or white.** Never on a book colour.
6. **Design the page for a 3-year-old, tell the story for a 5-year-old.** Big areas, few things, one change per page.
7. **Human-made.** Every line, letter and word in the book is made by a person. No AI images, no AI text, anywhere in the printed book.
8. **Calm and honest.** Flat colour, no effects. Only the claims allowed in the guideline, section 4.

## 6. Pim

The illustrator owns the drawing; Telning owns the character (section 18). The brief's rules (round seed-like creature, small shoot with two leaves, gender-neutral, not a known animal) still apply.

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Body | White fill, black line. Always. |
| Proportions | Head and body are one round shape, about 60% of the page height on a close-up. Eyes large and far apart. Mouth wide. Big enough for a 3-year-old to colour. |
| Faces (8) | calm, happy, surprised, worried, sad, angry, scared, proud. Each face is eyebrows + mouth + eyes, with the whole body helping (shaking lines, slump, stomp). |
| Poses (10) | standing, walking, sitting, reaching, hiding, hugging, stomping, lying down, pointing, jumping |
| Direction | Faces right or to the front. Enters from the left, moves right. Never faces left on a cover. |
| Position | Pim sits in a similar place on each page so a child finds it fast. |
| Consistency | Every page is checked against the model sheet. Pim never "drifts". |
| Leaves | Sage on covers, cards and posts. White inside the book. |
| On colour | Pim and any prop on a book colour must have a white or crayon fill. No open lines on a book colour. |
| Speaking | No speech bubbles with words. Thought bubbles with a picture inside are allowed and useful. |
| Others | A friend (same build, different shoot or ears) and one adult (taller, same line style). |

### The scribble (how "coloured in" looks on covers)
- Crayon strokes at about 25 degrees with small white gaps.
- Covers the left half of Pim's body with an uneven edge. The right half stays white.
- Inside the line, with one or two small slips. It should look like a child did it well.
- The illustrator draws one scribble texture by hand; it is reused as a clipped layer.

## 7. Line art for ages 3–7

| Item | Inside the book | On the cover |
|---|---|---|
| Line weight | 5–6 pt at final size (about 1.8–2.1 mm), the same on every page | 6–7 pt |
| Line colour | Black `#000000` | Black `#000000` |
| Ends and corners | Round | Round |
| Shapes | Every shape closed | Every shape closed |
| Fill | None (white) | White, or crayon scribble |
| Smallest area | 6 mm (a 3-year-old's crayon tip) | 6 mm |
| Elements | 1 character + 1–2 things per page | Pim + 2 props at most |
| White space | At least 40% of the page empty | — |
| Frames | 5 pt, corner radius 0.3 in | 6 pt, corner radius 0.3 in |

Why these numbers (guideline section 3.6 and 3.8): one picture at a time helps learning; clutter hurts attention; 3-year-olds scribble, so the picture must still read after a scribble.

The two tests every page must pass:
1. **Scribble test:** print the page, scribble over it with a crayon for ten seconds. Pim's face and the one change on the page must still be clear.
2. **Stranger test:** someone who has not seen the story tells it from the pages alone.

## 8. Story pictures: how the illustrator works

1. **Script first.** One line per picture: what we see, what Pim feels, what changes. 20–25 pictures. Order: setting (calm, Pim doing something well) → want → problem (feeling grows, shown in the body) → try (does not work) → help or pause (the fork) → fix → calm. See the brief, section 8.3.
2. **Thumbnails.** Small sketches of every page in one sheet. We check reading direction, one change per page, where Pim stands, and the mix of close-up, wide shot and centred vignette.
3. **Pencils.** Full-size rough drawings. Check against the model sheet.
4. **Inks.** Hand-inked or digitally inked with one brush, one weight. No shading, no hatching, no grey.
5. **Scan or export** at 600 dpi, pure black and white (threshold, no anti-aliasing), 8.5 x 8.5 in plus 0.125 in bleed only if something touches the edge. Vector (SVG or PDF) is welcome but not required.
6. **Closed-shape check.** Flood-fill test on every area.
7. **Scribble test and stranger test** on five pages before the rest are inked.

Rules for real things: in the doctor book, draw the stethoscope, the waiting room and the thermometer the way they really look. Pim can be a seed; the world around Pim is real.

## 9. Cover system

### 9.1 File size
- Trim 8.5 x 8.5 in. Bleed 0.125 in on every outside edge.
- Spine width = page count x 0.002252 in. 60 pages ≈ 0.135 in. Full cover ≈ 17.385 x 8.75 in (5216 x 2625 px at 300 dpi).
- Always download the KDP template for the final page count and shop, and check against it. No spine text under 79 pages.

### 9.2 One background across the whole cover
- Top band: cream, from the top edge to the hill line.
- Colour block ("the hill"): the book colour, from the hill line to the bottom and out to both edges.
- Hill line: 6 pt black, 3.40 in below the top trim at the edges and at the spine, rising to 3.15 in under Pim on the front. Flat on the back.
- Spine: no text, no icon. The background runs through.

### 9.3 Front cover
Measured from the top left corner of the front trim, in inches. Tune once in the first mockup with the illustrator, then lock.

| Part | Position | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Safe margin | 0.5 from every edge | No text or faces outside it |
| Brand line | x 0.6, y 0.5 to 0.82 | Logo mark + `telning` + "· a TADA story" (translated per edition) |
| Title, first line | x 0.6, baseline y 1.35 | "Pim Feels", Fredoka 600, 44 pt, charcoal |
| Title, key word | x 0.6, baseline y 2.50 | "Angry", Fredoka 600, 110 pt. Widest 7.3 in. |
| Small line | x 0.6, baseline y 2.85, widest 3.6 in | "A wordless colouring story", Nunito 700, 15 pt |
| Pim | centre x 4.9, y 5.55 | Body 3.5 in wide, hand-drawn, half scribbled. The shoot crosses the hill line into the cream band. |
| Props | x 0.6 to 7.9, y 4.0 to 7.5 | Two at most, drawn by the illustrator |
| Age pill | bottom left, 0.5 from the edges | 1.5 x 0.45 in, cream fill, 3 pt black line, "Ages 3–7", Fredoka 600, 16 pt |
| TADA badge | bottom right, centre x 7.35, y 7.35 | 1.3 in circle (section 11) |

Rules:
- Five text items at most: brand line, title, small line, age pill, badge.
- The key word is the biggest thing after Pim. It is the word the parent typed into the search box, so it changes per language ("Wütend", "Arg").
- The picture shows the strongest moment of the feeling, not the calm ending.
- The illustrator may add one hand-drawn background shape (a hill bump, a cloud) in sage. Nothing else.

### 9.4 Back cover
Measured from the top left corner of the back trim.

| Part | Position | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Tagline | x 0.6, baseline y 1.3 | "Stories your child finishes." Fredoka 600, 30 pt |
| Two lines about the book | x 0.6, y 1.7 to 2.4 | Nunito 400, 12.5 pt. What the story is about and what the child does. |
| Cream card | x 0.6 to 7.9, y 3.75 to 6.85 | Cream fill, 6 pt black line, radius 0.3 in |
| In the card, left | | Four lines: true picture count and "one picture per sheet, blank back"; "you decide" pages; page for grown-ups with the TADA way to talk; ages 3–7, no words to read |
| In the card, right | | Two small pages from the book, 1.5 in, white with line art |
| In the card, bottom | | "Created by the Telning team of cognitive and behavioural scientists." "Illustrated by [name]." One life-skill line. |
| Publisher | bottom left, x 0.5, y 7.3 to 7.9 | Logo lockup + "Telning Publishing House" in a cream pill |
| Barcode space | bottom right | About 2 x 1.2 in empty, 0.25 in from the bottom and the spine |

Say "feelings and life skills" on consumer covers. "SEL" and "CASEL" only in teacher materials.

### 9.5 What changes per book
Book colour; title words; Pim's face, pose and props; back-cover text and the two small pages. Everything else is locked.

### 9.6 What changes per language
Title words, small line, brand line suffix, age pill text, all back-cover text. Pictures, colours and positions never change.

### 9.7 Thumbnail test (before every upload)
1. Shrink the front cover to 160 px and 80 px wide.
2. Put it on white next to five competitor covers from the same Amazon shop.
3. A person who has never seen it must say within two seconds: for small children, the key word, a colouring book.
4. All Telning covers in one row must be easy to tell apart.

## 10. Inside the book and language editions

Paper white, ink black. Page 8.5 x 8.5 in, no bleed unless art touches the edge.

### 10.1 Layers
Every page file has two layers:
- **Art layer** (shared by all editions): drawings only. Never contains a word, a sign or a letter.
- **Text layer** (one per language): title page, imprint, parent page, feelings-check labels, last page, page numbers.

A new edition = the same art layer + a new text layer + a new cover text layer + a new ISBN.

### 10.2 Pages

| Page | Layout |
|---|---|
| Title page | Logo mark (black) top left. Title in Fredoka 600 in the cover's two-line style. "Telning" under it. "Illustrated by [name]". |
| Imprint page | Nunito 9 pt, bottom left. Publisher, copyright line, "All illustrations hand-drawn by [name]", printer line space. |
| For grown-ups | Margin 0.75 in. Heading Fredoka 22 pt. Four TADA rows: letter in a 0.6 in black circle, step name Fredoka 16 pt, two lines Nunito 12 pt, three model sentences each. The five questions. "Read it again." The marker tip. QR block bottom right. Text written by a person in each language. |
| Story picture | Right-hand page, blank back. Picture inside a 0.5 in margin. No frame. No text. |
| You decide (two endings) | Two frames side by side, each about 3.4 x 4.6 in, 0.4 in apart. A small hand-drawn pointing hand or arrow above, so the adult points to one at a time. Nothing says which ending is right. |
| You decide (draw) | One empty frame, about 6.5 x 6.5 in. Small Pim (pointing) bottom left, outside the frame. |
| Feelings check | Two rows of five faces in Pim's face style, each 1.3 in wide. Rows marked "before" and "after" with a sun and a moon icon as well as the words, so the page reads in any language. |
| Last page | Small Pim, one line ("Send Pim your ending"), QR block centred. |

### 10.3 QR block
- At least 1.25 in wide, pure black on white, clear white edge.
- Short web address under it in Nunito 700, 11 pt. One address per language edition.
- Test every printed QR code on the proof with two phones.

## 17. Illustrator brief and deliverables

### Deliverables for book 1
1. Pim model sheet: front, side and three-quarter views; 8 faces; 10 poses; the friend; the adult. One page with all of them at the same scale.
2. Logo mark, hand-drawn, then traced to vector.
3. 20–25 story pictures as in section 8.
4. 2–3 "you decide" spreads (two endings + the pointing cue) and the empty frame page with small Pim.
5. Ten feelings-check faces (five, twice) in Pim's face style.
6. Cover art: Pim at the strongest moment, two props, the scribble texture, one sage background shape.
7. Spot art: small Pim for the parent page and the last page; the story-card frame with small Pim.
8. Three process photos or scans (pencils, inks, desk) for social media and Amazon.
9. Source files (layered, 600 dpi or vector) and dated sketches for the authorship record.

### For books 2–5
Items 3 to 7 only, using the same model sheet. The illustrator may propose one new pose or face per book; we add it to the model sheet.

### Specs
- Line: one brush, one weight, 5–6 pt at 8.5 x 8.5 in. Round ends. No shading, no hatching, no grey, no texture inside the book.
- Shapes closed. Smallest area 6 mm. One character + 1–2 things per page. 40% of the page empty.
- Pure black and white at 600 dpi, or vector. Trim 8.5 x 8.5 in; add 0.125 in bleed only on pages where art touches the edge.
- No words, letters, signs or numbers inside any picture. Thought bubbles with pictures are fine.
- Real objects drawn realistically; Pim and friends stylised.
- Check every page against the model sheet before sending.

### Timeline for book 1 (fast plan)
Model sheet by 17 October. Thumbnails by 21 October. Five inked test pages by 24 October (scribble test, stranger test). All story pictures by 7 November. Spreads, faces and cover by 14 November. Fixes by 21 November.

### Reviews
One review call a week. Feedback in writing, page by page, against sections 6, 7 and 8 of this file.

## 18. Rights and the human-made rule

- Under Swedish law the illustrator keeps copyright unless the contract says otherwise. The contract must give WelloWork AB: ownership of the Pim character, the model sheet and the Telning logo; an exclusive worldwide licence (or transfer) for all series art in all media, languages and formats, including merchandise and the story card; and the right to hire other illustrators for later books using the model sheet. Pay fairly for this. Start from Svenska Tecknare's templates and have a Swedish lawyer read it. Moral rights stay with the illustrator: credit "Illustrated by [name]" everywhere.
- No AI images or AI text in any printed book, cover, Amazon image or Swedish submission. Claude is used for research, planning, checking and code (website, SVG tracing of the logo, file assembly), never for a drawing or a sentence that goes into the book.
- Keep the authorship record: scripts, thumbnails, dated sketches, drafts of the parent-page text with the writer's name, the contract. This is our proof for Amazon, for Swedish publishers and for copyright.

## 19. Do and do not

| Do | Do not |
|---|---|
| Put all text on cream or white | Put text on a book colour |
| One book colour per cover | Mix two book colours as backgrounds |
| Keep Pim white, half scribbled on covers | Give Pim a fixed body colour |
| Hand-drawn black line everywhere | Grey, coloured or AI-made outlines |
| Five text items on the front | Stars, bursts, "NEW", review quotes, study numbers |
| Flat colour | Gradients, shadows, textures, 3D |
| One picture per sheet | Two pictures facing each other (except the "you decide" spread, which gets a pointing cue) |
| Real objects drawn realistically | Talking furniture, cartoon medical tools |
| Show real pages in marketing | Show pages that are not in the book |
