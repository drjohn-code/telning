# Premium benchmark for Telning shop (Books & posters, Gifts)

Read on 12 Oct 2026. Facts only. "Not verified" means the page could not be read or did not show it. Prices are as shown on the page that day.

## (a) Summary

Premium children's brands sell the *moment*, not the object. The strongest shops (Tonies, Jellycat, Maileg, Moomin) put one product in use in the hero, name products in a fixed pattern (type + size + colour, or a first name + type), and repeat a short trust row (returns window, delivery days, payment logos, reviews count). Posters sell at about £12.50–£20 / €15–€20 for 70 × 100 cm, mugs at €16–€30 for 0.25–0.4 L, single cards at $6.50 with envelope, and card boxes at $32–$53. Review counts, a visible returns window (14–60 days), a named certificate (FSC, GOTS) and a stated paper weight are the trust signals that repeat. Packaging is part of the product at Maileg (the box is a bed) and Jellycat (drawstring bag, paid gift box, 70-character gift message). On Etsy, personalised items dominate mug and card bestseller lists; illustrated children's items are not in the top 10. Keyword data shows "colouring" is the UK form and "coloring" the US form, and that generic "coloring book" results are mostly cheap printable PDFs, so Telning must rank on long-tail phrases (wordless, child chooses the ending, giant colouring poster, Story Wall).

## (c) What sells, per product type

**Colouring posters.** Format: 70 × 100 cm landscape, folded into a printed pack, 120 gsm paper (OMY) or FSC paper (EggNogg). Price band £12.50–£20 / €15–€20 / $20–$29. Themes seen on bestseller or featured pages: cats, zoo and farm animals, ocean, dinosaurs, city or landmark (Paris, USA map), ABC and numbers, four seasons, space, teatime party, Christmas and Easter. Layout: one big scene with many small figures, so several children can each take a corner. Copy always lists three uses (floor, wall, tablecloth), "alone or with friends", and what to do after (frame, pin, wrap). Etsy adds name personalisation and classroom/party use. Licensed characters (Bluey, Minecraft) dominate the mass market, not the premium shops.

**Kids' book covers.** 2026 trend pieces point to hand-drawn, slightly imperfect art, visible pencil or paper texture, plain backgrounds, big title type and a cover that still reads at phone-thumbnail size. Series naming is strong: Phaidon "My Art Book of [Feeling]", Taschen "[Artist]. [Title]", Usborne "First Colouring [Theme]". Franchise-led titles top sales lists (Gruffalo), so a repeated mascot and series badge matter more than any single cover.

**Mugs.** Moomin top 10: 0.25–0.4 L, one character per mug, one short word in the name ("Love", "Hug", "Sleep Well"), blue most common, capacity in the name, gift box, dishwasher safe. Etsy top 10: personalised names, photos, engraving. Kids' mugs in retail are 170–280 ml, stoneware or porcelain, or unbreakable enamel/silicone for the youngest.

**Totes.** Premium kids' brands sell plain cotton totes with a soft colour name, GOTS stated, size in cm, €14. Trend guides (vendor-written, so not neutral) favour single-colour line art, clean type, and lifestyle photos over flat shots.

**Cards.** Birthday is the biggest occasion (Rifle: 76 of 328; Etsy: 4 of top 10). Winning motifs: cats, dogs, cake and sweets, florals; pink and green lead. Single card + envelope at $6.50; boxes of mixed designs $32–$53; UK kids' multipacks of 10 at £10–£15, 120 × 170 mm. Personalisation is UK-heavy: "personalised card" searches are 82% UK.

## (d) Rules for Telning (numbered)

1. **Six photos per product, in this order:** (1) front, flat, straight on; (2) in use, a child's hands colouring, no face needed; (3) scale shot next to a known object (hand, pencil box, chair); (4) close zoom of the line art; (5) the pack as it arrives; (6) posters: on the floor and on the wall. Benchmarks show 2–4 images; six beats them.
2. **One light, one background.** Daylight from one side, soft shadow, same off-white or warm grey surface on every product. Mugs also get one cut-out image on a plain background (Tonies style) for the listing grid.
3. **Prop list (max three per shot):** 3–5 loose coloured pencils, one crayon tin, masking tape on a wall, a wooden chair edge, a kitchen table corner, an envelope for cards, a mug of tea for the adult, the folded pack. No screens, no toys from other brands.
4. **Angles:** posters and cards top-down flat lay plus a 45° angle; mugs front with handle to the right, a three-quarter turn, and top-down to show the inside colour; books front cover, spine, one open spread.
5. **Name pattern:** "[Series] [Product type] [Size or count] – [Variant]". Example: "Pim's Forest Story Wall 70 × 100 cm – ages 3–5"; "Pim Mug 0,3 L – Forest"; "Greeting Cards, pack of 10 – Birthday". Size and age in the name, as Moomin (capacity), Liewood (colour) and Londji (format) do.
6. **Age in two bands everywhere:** 3–5 and 5–7 on cards, filters and names. Add "Gifts by age" in the menu (Liewood: 0–2, 2–4, 5–10).
7. **Price display:** one price, currency, "incl. VAT", and the delivery line directly under it ("Delivered in 3–5 days"). Show a free-shipping progress bar in the basket (Konges Sløjd). No struck-through prices at launch; justify price with facts: paper gsm, size, made/printed in [country], certificate.
8. **Trust row under "Add to basket", five items:** 30-day returns (Liewood, Wee Gallery, Tonies use 30), delivery days, payment logos (Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, Klarna; Swish for Sweden), certificate (FSC for paper, GOTS for totes), age-safety note.
9. **Reviews only when real.** Show the count and average once there are 20+ (Tonies "4.5 from 455"). Until then, use dated quotes from parents and teachers with first name and city.
10. **Spec block on every page:** size in cm, paper weight in gsm, page count, pens needed (OMY says 2–3 boxes of markers for a poster), dishwasher-safe for mugs, card size in mm and envelope colour.
11. **Packaging that is part of the play:** poster folded to about A5 in a printed sleeve that opens into a mini colouring page; mug in a printed box with a short Pim story inside the lid; cards banded with one spare "colour your own" card. Follow Maileg (box = bed) and Arabia (gift box).
12. **Gift options:** free gift message up to 70 characters; optional printed gift box for a small fee; one box per order (Jellycat pattern). Offer gift wrap as a product (Maileg).
13. **Hero:** one product in use, one headline, one button (Tonies, Liewood). Under it four tiles: Books, Story Walls, Gifts, By age. Show a product count on each tile (Wee Gallery).
14. **Story-moment line above the fold** on every product page, written with the formula in (e). Add one real parent quote under it when available.
15. **Badges only when true:** "New", "Bestseller", "Coming soon" (Moomin), "Last few". No "Sale" badge in the first six months.
16. **Bundles at full price:** "Complete the set" (book + Story Wall + mug) shown on every product page; add a small free gift in the basket (Konges Sløjd) instead of a discount.
17. **Email style:** subject under 50 characters, one story moment and one product per email, one button. Welcome email in Jellycat's tone ("Send me Jellycat joy!"). Sign-up offer: a free printable colouring page, not 10% off, to protect price.
18. **Card pack pages:** show all 10 designs in a grid, the envelope colour, the size in mm, "blank inside", and one card photographed in a hand.
19. **Spelling:** UK "colouring" on all EU and UK pages; US "coloring" once in the meta description and in alt text, never mixed in one headline.
20. **Never claim with adjectives.** Name the certificate (FSC, GOTS, OEKO-TEX), the paper weight and the place of printing. No print-partner names in customer copy.

## (e) The story-moment formula

**[Who, with age] + [where] + [with whom] + [the moment, ending in the choice].** One sentence, present tense, under 25 words, no adjectives before "child".

1. Book 3–5: "A three-year-old on the sofa with Mum colours the bear's path home and picks the brave ending."
2. Book 5–7: "After school at the kitchen table, a six-year-old reads the pictures to his sister and decides how it ends."
3. Story Wall: "Rainy Saturday, poster taped to the hallway wall: two cousins and Grandma each take a corner of the forest."
4. Mug: "Seven o'clock, warm milk in Pim's mug, while Dad asks which ending she chose today."
