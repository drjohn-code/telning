## Pages that rank, in short

- Generic "colouring book" terms: Etsy and Amazon listings, mostly cheap PDFs. Hard to win; use for meta only.
- "giant colouring poster": brand shops (Okido), museum shops (Tate), UK makers (EggNogg), Etsy. A product page with size, paper and uses can rank.
- "wordless picture books" and "decision making": lists and teacher articles. A guide page with a real list wins here, not a shop page.
- "children's mug", "children's greeting cards": retailers with spec-heavy pages (capacity, size in mm, material). Match that format.
